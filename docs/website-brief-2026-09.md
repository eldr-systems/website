## 2. Positioning

> **We turn a remote measurement problem into a working, low-power, connected device with a data backend.**

**Who it's for:** companies that need to monitor something in the field but have no electronics or firmware team of their own, and companies whose cellular devices have battery or connectivity problems.

**Decision makers reading the site:** CTOs, heads of hardware/embedded, firmware leads, IoT product managers. Many are Swedish, Danish or German engineers reading in a second language.

**Lead market (what the site headlines):** companies with battery-powered LTE-M, NB-IoT or 2G/3G devices in the field. No domain network needed, sells abroad, urgent and measurable pain (2G/3G shutdowns are forcing migrations).

**Growth market (named on the site as a focus):** energy and electrical monitoring
- solar and battery installers
- EV-charger and heat-pump companies
- energy management startups
- building operators

**What sets us apart**
1. **End to end.** PCB, firmware, cellular connectivity, backend and data analysis in one small team.
2. **Low-power cellular IoT.** NB-IoT and LTE-M, battery budgets, PSM/eDRX, poor coverage, OTA updates.
3. **Electrical know-how.** We measure voltage, current and power safely near mains.
4. **Finding faults in device data.** Anomaly detection, forecasting and fault detection on sensor time series. Say "we find faults in your device data", never "AI solutions".
5. **Trained in Scandinavia, based in Budapest.** Uppsala and Aalborg master's degrees, working English, Hungarian cost base.

**Backend and ML** appear as parts of the end-to-end package, never as standalone services. The headline is always field devices, low-power cellular and energy monitoring.

**What we are not:** a web/app agency, an AI consultancy, or a large PCB design house.

## 3. Voice and copy rules

**Personality:** calm, precise, direct, quietly competent. The feeling: *the engineers you'd trust near mains voltage.*

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Specific | Vague or hype-driven |
| Sober and practical | Flashy |
| Small and personal | A faceless outsourcing shop |
| Honest about what we know | Overclaiming seniority or scale |

**Rules**
1. **Numbers over adjectives.** "8 years on two AA cells", not "ultra-efficient".
2. **Name the technology.** NB-IoT, LTE-M, PSM, eDRX, OTA.
3. **Plain English, short sentences.** Second-language readers.
4. **Show experience through specifics, not years.** "We've debugged NB-IoT attach failures on three operators."
5. **Be clear about scope, price and process.** Nordic buyers value predictability.
6. **Honest scarcity only.** "Three engineers; we take two audits a month." No countdowns or fake urgency.

**Banned:** revolutionary, cutting-edge, next-generation, game-changing, AI-powered solutions, synergy, seamless, world-class, leverage (as a verb).

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Cutting-edge IoT solutions" | "Battery-powered sensors that report over NB-IoT for years" |
| "AI-powered insights" | "We flag faulty inverters from their own data" |
| "End-to-end digital transformation" | "From circuit board to dashboard" |
| "Experienced team of experts" | "Three engineers: electronics, firmware and connectivity, backend and data" |

10. **Contact.** A named person's email (Vencel), not info@, plus a booking link.
11. **Footer.** Company name, registered seat, company registration number, tax number, EU VAT number, GDPR privacy notice.

## 6. Offers

**(conflict)** The Brand Identity file lists three website offers (Proof-of-concept sprint, Battery and connectivity rescue, Device data analytics). The newer Offers file defines a fuller ladder with the Audit as the lead offer. **Recommendation:** use the Offers file. Show the Audit first and prominently, then two or three build offers. "Battery and connectivity rescue" becomes the Audit + Fix implementation pair.

**Suggested cards for launch:** A (lead, featured) · B · C · D. Mention E and F briefly. Do **not** publish G until it's validated.

### A. Battery and Connectivity Audit (lead offer, feature it)

**Promise:** In 2 weeks, for a fixed price, we find out why your cellular devices drain their batteries or drop offline, and which fixes to make first.

**For:** companies with battery-powered LTE-M, NB-IoT or 2G/3G devices in the field and no dedicated cellular expert.

**Symptoms (good as a checklist on the page):**
- Rated battery life isn't reached
- Devices go offline on certain operators or in poor coverage
- Field performance is much worse than on the bench
- Unsure whether PSM/eDRX actually work
- OTA updates avoided because they feel risky

**Process:** 20-min scoping call → you send 3–5 devices → week 1: power traces and network tests → week 2: analysis and fix estimates → report and 1-hour walkthrough. **Your effort: about 2 hours in total.**

**You provide:** 3–5 devices with current firmware; modem configuration or firmware read access; field logs if available; operators and countries.

**You get:**
- Power profile per device state
- Battery life today, and after each proposed fix
- Network test results (attach, reconnect, data transfer; good and poor coverage)
- Ranked fix list with gain, effort and risk
- Our measurement scripts
- 1-hour walkthrough, plus a 30-minute follow-up call after 30 days

**Guarantee:** If we don't find a 20%+ battery gain or a connectivity fix, there is no fee. (Conditional on receiving the required devices and information; state this in small print.)

**Price:** €7,500 fixed, excl. VAT. Fee credited in full against fix implementation booked within 60 days.

**Capacity:** two audits per month.

**Not included:** implementing fixes, hardware redesign, certification, on-site visits (quoted separately at a fixed price).

### B. Fix implementation
Implement the audit's fixes, with before/after traces and an OTA rollout to a test group.
**Guarantee:** the agreed gain, or we keep working at no extra cost. **Indicative:** €15–40k fixed.

### C. 2G/3G migration sprint
Move a device to LTE-M/NB-IoT: module choice, firmware port, power budget, operator test report. Built for shutdown deadlines. **Indicative:** €20–50k.

### D. Field prototype in 6 weeks
Working field devices, dashboard, unit cost at volume, certification roadmap, source code.
**Guarantee:** weekly demos; if we slip a milestone, the next week is free. **Indicative:** €25–45k.

### E. Edge AI feasibility sprint
Train a fault-detection model on your data, fit it to your microcontroller, and measure accuracy, memory and power. 4 weeks. **Indicative:** €15–25k.

### F. Fleet care (recurring)
Monitoring, OTA rollouts, SIM management, monthly fault report, response time for critical issues. Per device per month plus a base fee. Offered at the end of every build.

### G. Fleet health for installers (SaaS)
Inverter data in, weekly list of underperforming sites. **Not for the website yet — validate first.**

**Pricing display (to decide):** whether to show "from" prices on the site. Prices above are assumptions still being tested in conversations. The Audit is the strongest candidate for a published price, because a fixed, visible price is what makes it an easy first buy.

**Free first step (optional CTA):** "Send one power trace and your modem configuration; we'll send three observations within 48 hours."

- Lowercase **eldr** wordmark, "systems" set smaller.
