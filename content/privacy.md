## What this website collects

Nothing. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts,
and serves its fonts from its own domain. There is no tracking to opt out of, which is
why there is no cookie banner.

You do not have to take our word for it: the site is built from a public repository at
[github.com/eldr-systems](https://github.com/eldr-systems), and every build checks that
no third-party asset request has crept in.

## What our host sees

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records request logs
including IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We have no access to those
logs and cannot identify visitors from them. GitHub's privacy practices are their own.

## If you email us or book a call

We keep what you send us so we can reply, and, if the conversation goes further, so we
can scope and quote the work. We do not sell it, we do not share it with anyone outside
the three of us, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as it is commercially useful, and delete it
on request.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be
corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to our holding it. Write to us and we
will answer.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Hungarian
National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).
