## What you get

- The ranked fixes implemented, highest gain first
- Before-and-after power traces for each one, measured the same way
- An OTA rollout to a test group before the fleet

## The guarantee

We reach the gain agreed in the scoping document, or we keep working at no extra cost.

## What we need from you

The audit, or an equivalent set of measurements. We will not implement fixes against a
battery estimate nobody has measured.
