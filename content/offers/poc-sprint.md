## What you get

- A working device measuring what you need, on your bench or on a real site
- Full source code and schematics, yours to keep
- A simple dashboard showing live and historical data
- A battery-life estimate measured from a real power-profiler trace, not calculated from a datasheet

## How it runs

Four to eight weeks, depending on how many quantities you need to measure and whether
the site has mains power. Weekly demos with working hardware, not a status report.

We start with the measurement problem, not the enclosure. Getting voltage and current
right near mains is where the risk is; everything downstream is easier to change later.

## What we need from you

One technical contact, a description of what is being measured and where, and access to
a representative site if the connectivity is uncertain. If coverage at the site is
unknown, we test it before committing to NB-IoT or LTE-M.
