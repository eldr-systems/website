## What you get

- A power profile of each device, broken down by state
- Battery life as it is today, and as it would be after each proposed fix
- Network test results — attach, reconnect and data transfer, in good coverage and poor
- A ranked list of fixes, each with its gain, its effort and its risk
- Our measurement scripts, yours to keep
- A one-hour walkthrough, and a 30-minute follow-up call after 30 days

## The guarantee

If we find neither a 20% battery gain nor a connectivity fix, there is no fee. This holds
provided we receive the devices and information agreed at the scoping call.

The fee is credited in full against a fix implementation booked within 60 days.

## Who this is for

Companies with battery-powered LTE-M, NB-IoT or 2G/3G devices in the field and no
dedicated cellular expert. The symptoms are familiar:

- Rated battery life is never reached
- Devices go offline on certain operators, or in poor coverage
- Field performance is far worse than the bench predicted
- Nobody can say whether PSM and eDRX are actually working
- OTA updates are avoided because they feel risky

## What we need from you

Three to five devices running your current firmware, the modem configuration or read
access to the firmware, field logs if you have them, and the operators and countries
involved.

Your own effort is about two hours in total.
