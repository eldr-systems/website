## What you get

- Module selection, with the trade-offs written down rather than asserted
- The firmware ported, with the cellular stack reworked for PSM and eDRX
- A power budget for the new design, measured rather than calculated
- An operator test report covering attach, reconnect and transfer

## Who this is for

Anyone holding a deployed fleet on a network with a published shutdown date. The deadline
is set by the operator, which makes this the one piece of work that cannot be deferred.

## What we need from you

The current device and its firmware source, the countries and operators it runs on, and
the shutdown dates you are working against.
